OpenFilament CFS is an independent, open-source Android tool for preparing, identifying and managing third-party filament spools used with compatible Creality CFS-equipped printers.

Simple Mode keeps the workflow plug-and-play: choose the filament, colour and spool size. Expert Mode exposes mapping evidence, diagnostics and capability-gated printer integrations.

The application is local-first and does not require an account. MIFARE Classic support depends on the NFC controller in your phone. Printer and firmware compatibility varies.

OpenFilament CFS is not affiliated with or endorsed by Creality.
